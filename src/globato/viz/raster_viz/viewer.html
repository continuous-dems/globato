2D: wheel to zoom, drag to pan. Both panels remain geographically synchronized. NoData is transparent. RGB is selectable only as a background, not an elevation product. Adjust RGB transparency and elevation-layer opacity independently in each panel. Use Boundaries to select the outlines you want to compare.

3D: drag to rotate, Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Terrain and sparse cells are displayed automatically. NoData remains empty; no elevations are invented. RGB imagery is drawn on its own georeferenced plane underneath terrain, covering the full image footprint even where terrain is NoData. RGB transparency and terrain opacity are separate controls; the imagery does not alter terrain heights. Vector overlays are 2D only.
